## 7. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (CURRENT)

These FAQs appear on the **Contact** page and represent valuable legal education content to carry over:

**Q: What is an Ante-nuptial Contract?**
> The ANC is an agreement entered between two parties prior to marriage; which then makes your marriage out of community of property.

**Q: I am still young, do I need a will?**
> The common misconception is that a will is a document that prepares you for death. You are merely setting out your wishes and intentions, ensuring that your legacy is carried with purpose even after life. As long as one is 16 years and older and mentally capable of understanding the nature and effects of one's actions, then a will is recommended.

**Q: Is a verbal agreement enforceable?**
> Yes, although it will be hard to prove all the elements of a valid contract as there is no written agreement. An exception applies in a sale and purchase of property agreement.

**Q: Does lobola without registration of the marriage with the Home Affairs make me married in community of property?**
> Yes. You and your partner must sign an ante-nuptial contract before the lobola is negotiated.

**Q: I am married in community of property, is it possible to change my marital regime to out of community of property?**
> Yes, it is possible through a Section 21(1) of the Matrimonial Property Act 88 of 1984 application with the High Court.
